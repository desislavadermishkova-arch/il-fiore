Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these paintings originals or prints?

Every piece in the shop is an original, one-of-a-kind watercolour painting on paper — not a print or reproduction.

Do you take commissions?

Yes. Get in touch with a description of what you're looking for, a rough size, and your timeline, and you'll get a reply with availability and pricing.

How do I buy a painting?

Click Inquire on any painting in the shop, or send a message through the form above naming the piece you're interested in. You'll get a reply with price and availability.

Do you ship internationally?

Shipping is arranged on a case-by-case basis for each piece. Mention your location when you inquire and you'll get shipping cost and timing along with the price.
